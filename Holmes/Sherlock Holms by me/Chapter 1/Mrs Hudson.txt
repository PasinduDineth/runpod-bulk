THE ADVENTURE OF THE NINTH HOUR
CHAPTER I: THE GENTLEMAN WHO KNEW THE HOUR
MRS. HUDSON — DIALOGUE

[Voice Description: Elderly, courteous, and slightly breathless with concern. Mrs. Hudson stands in the Baker Street doorway with an alarmed visitor downstairs. Keep her delivery unhurried but let worry make her voice quaver faintly.]
“Mr. Holmes, sir, there's a gentleman downstairs. He says he must see you before the hour is out. I told him you were engaged, but he looked so very ill that I hadn't the heart to send him away.”
