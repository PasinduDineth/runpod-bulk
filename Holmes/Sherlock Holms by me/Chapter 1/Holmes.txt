THE ADVENTURE OF THE NINTH HOUR
CHAPTER I: THE GENTLEMAN WHO KNEW THE HOUR
SHERLOCK HOLMES — DIALOGUE

[Voice Description: Crisp, lively, and intellectually mischievous. In the quiet sitting room, pose the question as a puzzle and lightly stress “exact hour” and “death,” enjoying Watson's reaction without sounding theatrical.]
“Watson, if a man tells you the exact hour of his death, what would you ask him?”

[Voice Description: Brisk and sparkling with playful certainty. Holmes anticipates the visitor and shares a knowing, sly prediction with Watson.]
“Then our new visitor is unlikely to be disappointed in either of us.”

[Voice Description: Bright, decisive, and alert. Holmes takes command while Mrs. Hudson is troubled; ask for the lamp with practical purpose and contained curiosity.]
“Show him up, Mrs. Hudson. And bring another lamp. We shall need to see his face.”

[Voice Description: Quick-minded and politely ironic, welcoming but sharply investigative. Holmes tests the distressed visitor's confidence and asks who arranged the appointment.]
“You speak with a remarkable degree of certainty, Mr. Whitmore. Who has promised you this unhappy appointment?”

[Voice Description: Measured and ominous while reading the threat, then pivot into dry, sparkling disdain at the theatrical writer's authorship. Do not overplay the menace.]
“‘When the ninth hour sounds, Edward Whitmore shall fall in the room he has locked against the world. No hand shall touch him, and no door shall open.’ A theatrical writer, our correspondent. And unusually particular about the room.”

[Voice Description: Begin with calm agreement, then turn abruptly probing. Emphasize “Robert Keene” just enough to test Whitmore's response; retain brisk, controlled curiosity.]
“Then we had better make haste. One question first: why is the name Robert Keene so unpleasant to you?”

[Voice Description: Precise and decisive, with lively forward momentum. Holmes dismisses the evasion, directs Watson to come, and ends as they prepare to investigate.]
“Everything that a frightened man dismisses as unimportant deserves our attention. Watson, your coat.”
